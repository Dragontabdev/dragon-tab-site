Features

Dragon Tab strips your Android workflow down to the essentials and polishes every one of them — no clutter, no bloat, no surprises.

Multi-Instance Engine

Open several Android sessions side by side, each with its own device and its own window. Switch, resize and keep them all alive at once.

Real-Time Mirroring

Low-latency screen mirroring straight from your device. Touch on the mirror, watch it respond on the phone — instantly.

Smart Keyboard Mapping

Bind mouse and keyboard shortcuts to any mirror. The mapping stays locked behind a PIN at every launch, so nobody touches your setup.

Workspaces

Save a full group of sessions — order, layout and all — then restore it in one click. Your routine reopens exactly where you left it.

Media Hub

YouTube and Spotify live inside the app, organised per workspace. Keep the soundtrack next to the instances that need it.

Built-In Guides

Guides, daily almanax and a dungeon planner ship with the app. The reference you always tabbed away for is one rail click away.

How It Works

Up and running in three steps

No accounts. No configuration files. No terminal commands.

  1. 01

    Install

    Download the portable build and launch it. There is no installer, no admin prompt and no background service left behind.

  2. 02

    Configure

    Plug in your Android device with USB debugging enabled, then pick your workspace, keyboard mapping and unlock PIN.

  3. 03

    Control

    Mirror, switch and drive every instance from one window. Restore your saved workspace next time and you are back in seconds.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Still curious? Everything below is straight from the app's behaviour.

Dragon Tab is a Windows desktop application that runs several Android sessions side by side, mirrors each screen in real time and lets you drive them from a single, lightweight interface.

There is no artificial cap in the app. The real limit is your machine: each instance decodes a live video stream, so RAM, CPU and USB bandwidth decide how far you can go. Most laptops handle three to five instances comfortably.

No. Dragon Tab works with standard ADB debugging and scrcpy-style video streaming, both of which run on a stock, unrooted Android device.

Any phone or tablet that exposes USB debugging through ADB is supported. A wired USB connection is recommended for latency; wireless ADB works on devices that allow it.

Keyboard mapping is locked at every startup. Enter your PIN once per launch to unlock shortcuts. The default PIN for a new install is 1234567890, and only a SHA-256 fingerprint of it is stored on disk — never the PIN itself.

The portable build is a free download. One executable, no installer and no background services — keep it on a USB stick if you like.

Yes. Dragon Tab ships as a portable x64 build for Windows 10 and Windows 11 and runs without an installation step.

Sessions, workspaces and preferences are saved in your Windows user profile, so updating or moving the executable never wipes them. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
