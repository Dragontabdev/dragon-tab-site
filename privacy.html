Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Short version: Dragon Tab is local-first. It has no accounts, no telemetry and no tracking, and the data it creates stays on your computer.

1. Information we do not collect

Dragon Tab does not require an account and does not collect personal information. The application does not transmit usage analytics, crash reports, device identifiers or your Android screen content to us or to any third party.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything the app remembers is written to your Windows user profile and never uploaded:

  • Session and workspace definitions — device identifiers, tab order and layout.
  • Keyboard mapping preferences and your last enabled state.
  • A SHA-256 fingerprint of your mapping PIN in mapping-pin.json. The PIN itself is never stored in plain text and is never sent to the interface layer.
  • Media bookmarks, playlists and timestamps associated with your workspaces.

3. USB debugging and device access

Mirroring uses Android Debug Bridge (ADB) over USB or local Wi-Fi. The connection is established directly between your computer and your own device. No video stream, input event or screenshot passes through a remote server.

4. Third-party content

The built-in media tab can display YouTube and Spotify web content. When you use those tabs, the corresponding services load in their own context and are governed by their own privacy policies and cookie practices. If you never open them, no request is made.

5. This website

This website is a static page. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Web fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends a standard HTTP request — including your IP address — to Google's servers to load them. The download link serves the application package directly from this site's hosting.

6. Children

Dragon Tab is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Your control

You can erase everything the app stores by deleting its data folder in your Windows user profile. Uninstalling is a matter of deleting the portable executable.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too. Material changes will be described here.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us through the contact page.
